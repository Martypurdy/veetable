Layout notes

    How the VeeTable system fits together
    • 64 mm Vee grid. Every Vee is 90°, 4 mm deep and 8 mm wide at the surface. Bins sit on whole 64 mm cells.
    • 96 mm dog holes. 96 mm is 1.5 cells, so holes alternate between a Vee line and a cell centre. The two patterns line up every 192 mm.
    • Centred holes. A cell count divisible by 3 (6, 9, 12, 18…) can't centre the most holes, so one side ends up half a cell further in. Go one cell up or down, or choose "Always centred".
    • Holes stay inside the frame. No hole centre is closer than 32 mm to the outer Vee.
    • Cut order. Drill the dog holes and fixing holes first, then cut the Vees.
